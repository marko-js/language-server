"@marko/language-tools": patch
---

Type and check attribute tags that are not written once under their tag: repeated, or in an `<if>`, `<for>` or `<while>`. An attribute they do not have or a wrong value is reported at that attribute (or, for a whole group, at its first attribute tag), and a missing required one at the attribute tag; a template whose `Input` is `unknown` takes none. A generic tag infers its type parameters from them as from its attributes, also from a loop's values (eg `<pick selected=1>` with a `<for>` of numbers infers `number`), so its own params and handlers are typed by them too. Their body params are typed by the tag's input: by a generic tag's type parameters where they are written directly under it, repeated or in an `<if>`; `any` in a loop's body, an `<if>` with a scriptlet, or an attribute tag's own repeated attribute tags (eg in `<data-table rows=users><for|c| of=cols><@column|row|>`, `row` is `any` rather than `unknown`). A required attribute tag counts as given by an attribute tag of its name anywhere under the tag (or under its attribute tag), also one that not every render gives (eg only in an `<if>`, or in a `<for to=3>`, which was reported as missing); one given nowhere is still reported at the tag.
